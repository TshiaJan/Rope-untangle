# Privacy Policy for Rope Untangle Puzzle

**Effective Date:** September 17, 2026  
**Developer:** Tshiamo Jantjie  
**Contact:** tshiajan@gmail.com  

Thank you for playing **Rope Untangle Puzzle** ("the Game", "we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices regarding information collection, usage, and disclosure when you play our game.

---

### 1. Information We Do Not Collect
- **No Personal Identifiable Information (PII):** We do not require you to create an account, register, or provide personal details such as your real name, email address, physical address, phone number, or payment card information.
- **No Precise Location Tracking:** We do not track, collect, or request access to your GPS or precise physical location.
- **No Contact List or Device Storage Snooping:** We do not access your contacts, private photos, camera, or microphone.

---

### 2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device
The Game operates primarily client-side and stores your gameplay progress and preferences locally on your device using standard browser/application local storage (`localStorage`). This data includes:
- **Game Progression:** Current level reached, highest level completed, and star ratings.
- **Pretend Virtual Economy:** Token balance and star counts collected through gameplay (virtual tokens have no real-world monetary value).
- **Player Preferences:** Sound effects on/off toggles and initial tutorial completion flags.

*Note: This data remains solely on your device. Clearing your application cache or uninstalling the Game will reset this local progress.*

---

### 3. Advertising & Third-Party Services
The Game is designed to be fully playable offline. If connected to the internet, optional third-party advertising networks (such as Google AdMob or Unity Ads) may be used to serve non-intrusive or optional rewarded video ads (e.g., earning extra in-game tokens).

These third-party ad networks may automatically collect certain pseudonymous, non-personally identifiable diagnostic data in accordance with their respective privacy policies, including:
- Mobile Advertising Identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID or Apple IDFA).
- Device make, model, operating system version, and general network connection type.
- Ad interaction statistics (e.g., ad views and completions).

You can reset or opt out of personalized ad tracking anytime via your mobile device settings (*Settings > Google > Ads* on Android or *Settings > Privacy > Tracking* on iOS).

---

### 4. Children’s Privacy (COPPA Compliance)
**Rope Untangle Puzzle** is a general-audience casual brain-teaser puzzle game suitable for players of all ages. We do not knowingly collect any personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in certain jurisdictions). If you are a parent or guardian and believe that personal data has inadvertently been provided, please contact us immediately at **tshiajan@gmail.com**, and we will promptly remove it.

---

### 5. Data Security
Because we do not maintain external user databases or collect personal user accounts, the risk of external data exposure is minimal. Your gameplay state is protected by standard operating system sandboxing on your device.

---

### 6. Updates to This Privacy Policy
We may periodically update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our practices or applicable regulatory standards. Any changes will be posted here with an updated "Effective Date."
